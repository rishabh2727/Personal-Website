# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Rishabh Mehra for new-grad backend or full-stack software engineering roles. Engineering leads and technical reviewers are a secondary audience when they follow project links or inspect implementation details.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio should quickly establish who Rishabh is, the kind of engineering work he does, and why his experience is relevant. A successful visit leads both to deeper exploration of his projects and to contact about an interview.

## Positioning

Rishabh is a recent University of Alberta Computer Science graduate who values reliable, understandable systems over demo-only polish. His work connects backend fundamentals with practical full-stack delivery, supported by co-op experience in research computing, IT operations, and software engineering.

## Operating Context

Visitors will usually scan the site quickly on a laptop or phone while comparing candidates. They need a concise introduction, credible experience signals, readable project summaries, direct GitHub links, a simple skills overview, and obvious contact paths.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site is a responsive single-page React and Vite application.
- It must present these four projects: Care Companion (Notty), Core Banking Ledger, Social Distribution, and AI Document Chat.
- It should make Rishabh's University of Alberta education and co-op background visible without turning the site into a full résumé.
- Contact details, project links, and technical claims must remain factual.
- Future work must not invent employers, impact metrics, testimonials, project outcomes, or technologies.
- The writing should stay short, direct, honest, and personal rather than corporate.
- The experience should work well on phones and laptops without heavy animation.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Rishabh Mehra.
- Voice: thoughtful, practical, technically curious, and plainspoken.
- The portfolio should communicate care for correctness, useful software, and continued learning.
- Preserve the current contact identity and public GitHub project links unless Rishabh supplies replacements.

## Evidence on Hand

- The repository contains the current portfolio copy and implementation.
- Public source repositories are linked for all four featured projects.
- Confirmed background includes a University of Alberta Computer Science degree and co-op experience in research computing, IT operations, and software engineering.
- No testimonials, client logos, press, awards, or externally verified product metrics are available and none should be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. Make the candidate and role fit clear within the first screen.
2. Lead with specific, credible work rather than broad claims.
3. Reward technical curiosity with concise project detail and direct source links.
4. Keep every interaction simple enough for a fast hiring review.
5. Preserve Rishabh's natural voice and factual accuracy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic structure, visible keyboard focus, sufficient color contrast, readable type sizes, comfortable touch targets, and reduced-motion support. The complete experience must remain usable on both phone and laptop layouts.
